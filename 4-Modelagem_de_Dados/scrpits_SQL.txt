Baseado no esquema ER

- Produtos mais vendidos (top 10 a partir de 2024) :

  select orders.product_id,  products.name as produto, count(*) as total_vendido
  from orders
  left join products on orders.product_id =  products.id
  where orders.date_partiton >= '2024-01-01'
  group by 1,2
  order by 3 desc
  limit 10

- Listar histórico de compras de um cliente especifíco :

  select orders.*
  from orders
  left join customers on orders.customer_id = customers.id
  where customers.name = ‘Pedro Inoue‘
  and customers.id = '123'
  order by sales.created_at desc

- Calcular o faturamento mensal :

  select date_format(created_at, ‘yyyy-MM’) as mes, sum(total_value) as faturamento
  from orders
  group by 1
  order by 1 desc
